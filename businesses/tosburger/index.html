Good to know

A few useful answers.

What is the learning approach?+

Read a model answer, learn reusable expression blocks, then assemble and speak an answer for the situation.

Does practice guarantee a test result?+

No. Progress depends on the learner and their practice. AI feedback is for practice, not an official ETS result.
